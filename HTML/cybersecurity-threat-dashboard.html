Global Cybersecurity Threat Dashboard

Interactive analysis and visualization of global cybersecurity threats to inform strategic risk management and defense mechanisms.

📋 Project Overview

This project presents an interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze and visualize global cybersecurity threats. The dashboard transforms raw threat data into actionable visual insights, providing a comprehensive overview of the evolving cybersecurity landscape worldwide.

By examining attack vectors, geographic vulnerabilities, and threat severity patterns across different countries and industries, this project identifies critical risk areas and suggests defense mechanisms that organizations can adopt. The analysis is grounded in real-world cyber incident data spanning multiple years, offering both strategic and tactical insights for cybersecurity decision-making.

This project was undertaken as part of my MBA program, combining data analytics with cybersecurity risk management principles.

🔍 Key Analytical Insights

Geographic Vulnerabilities

Identification of high-risk countries and regions with elevated cyber threat exposure, analyzing attack frequency and severity patterns across geographies.

Industry Risk Assessment

Analysis of cyber threat distribution across different industries, identifying which sectors face the greatest exposure to specific attack vectors.

Attack Vector Analysis

Examination of threat types and attack patterns, revealing prevalent threats and their temporal trends to support proactive defense strategies.
