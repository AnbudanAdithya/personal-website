Bike Sales Analytics Dashboard

Identifying key drivers of bike purchase decisions using logistic regression in R and Power BI visualisation.

📋 Project Overview

This business analytics project analyses a dataset of 1,000 bike sales records to uncover the demographic and financial factors that most strongly influence whether a customer purchases a bike. The analysis pipeline runs end-to-end from raw data cleaning to a fully visualised Power BI dashboard.

This project was undertaken as part of my business analytics course during my MBA program

The core model is a logistic regression built in R, which predicts the probability of a bike purchase based on variables such as income, age, commute distance, marital status, and region. The results are then visualised in Power BI to surface actionable insights for marketing and sales strategy.

🔍 Key Analytical Areas

Income Analysis

Examining how customer income levels correlate with bike purchase likelihood across different demographics.

Commute Distance

Analysing whether proximity to work drives bike adoption — a key variable in the logistic model.

Age & Demographics

Segmenting purchase behaviour by age group, marital status, and number of children.

Regional Trends

Comparing purchase rates across North America, Europe, and Pacific regions to identify market opportunities.
