LET ME INTRODUCE MYSELF

I'm someone who runs on curiosity more than anything else. Whether it's a financial model, a song I'm composing at midnight, or a novel I picked up on a whim — I learn differently each time, and I think that's the point. I'm currently pursuing my MBA at SSSIHL, majoring in Finance with a minor in Analytics, but that's context, not identity.

At my core, I'm probably best described as honest and grounded, with a tendency to think deeper than the situation requires. I care genuinely about the people around me — not as a soft skill, but as something that actually shapes how I work and who I show up as.

"I care a lot about getting to the 'why' behind things — why companies grow or collapse, why a piece of music hits differently, why some journeys stay with you long after they end. I believe the journey is where the real learning happens."

Outside of work, music is almost always on — not as background noise, but as a constant companion. I also compose my own tracks, which is its own kind of thinking. I read thrillers (Dan Brown and David Baldacci are permanent fixtures), write occasionally on Substack, and spend a good amount of time simply exploring whatever seems interesting that week

"Excellence isn't a destination you arrive at. It's what happens when you stay honest with yourself along the way."

~ S. Adithya
